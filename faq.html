FAQ

Questions, answered.

What is tech literacy?

It is knowing how to use technology confidently and safely: computers, the internet, and tools like AI.

Why does it matter?

Jobs, school, banking, health care, and government services increasingly happen online. Tech skills also help you stay safe from scams.

How fast is technology changing?

Quickly. AI, automation, and digital services keep changing how people work and learn, so learning how to learn tech matters more than any single tool.

What is the digital divide?

The gap between people who have access to technology and skills and people who don't. Cost, location, and lack of training all contribute.

Do I need to be good at math?

No. Most beginner tech skills need curiosity and practice, not advanced math.

Do I need an expensive computer?

No. Our lessons run in a web browser on most laptops, and many libraries offer free computers and internet.

How does AI affect jobs?

AI changes some tasks and creates new ones. People who understand AI and can check its work are better prepared for those changes.

How does AI affect learning?

It can be a helpful tutor or brainstorming tool, but it can also be wrong. Follow your teacher's rules and do your own thinking.

How do I spot misinformation?

Check who made it, look for other trusted sources, and be cautious when something is shocking or urgent.

What are deepfakes?

AI-made images, voices, or videos that look real. Check the source before you believe or share them.

Is TechScale free?

Yes. Lessons, quizzes, and challenges are free, with no account needed.

How do I volunteer?

Fill out the form on the Volunteer page.

How do I request a class?

Use the Request a Class page and tell us your ZIP code and topic.
